London is vegan easy mode: every chain has a labeled vegan menu, every
supermarket has an own-brand plant range, and nobody blinks at a toddler
in a café. The challenge isn't food — it's your wallet.

## Where to stay

We picked **east London** (around Victoria Park) over the center: flat
canal-side walks for the stroller, the best coffee density in the city,
and a Sunday flower market that doubles as free toddler entertainment.

## The eating shortlist

- **Breakfast:** any specialty café — oat flat whites are the default
  here, not the exception. Babyccinos are free half the time.
- **Lunch:** supermarket meal deals are the budget hack. Boots and Tesco
  both label vegan clearly; a picnic in the park beats any restaurant
  with a toddler anyway.
- **Dinner:** the fully vegan chains (and the vegan menus at every pizza
  and curry place) mean you can go a whole week without repeating.

## The free stuff

Every major museum is free. The Natural History Museum's dinosaur hall is
worth approximately four hours of toddler awe, and there's a decent
vegan-labeled café inside when the awe runs out.

## The verdict

**Difficulty: 1/5.** The easiest trip we've done. Budget is the only boss
fight — picnic lunches and free museums are how you win it.
