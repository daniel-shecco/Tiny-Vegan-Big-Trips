Copenhagen is what happens when an entire city agrees that design, coffee,
and children all matter. Rent a cargo bike on day one — it's the local
parenting uniform and genuinely the best way to move a toddler here.

## Where to stay

**Nørrebro.** It's the most interesting food neighborhood, the
playgrounds are works of art (literally — look up the themed ones), and
you're a ten-minute pedal from everything.

## The eating shortlist

- **Breakfast:** the bakery situation is elite, and most now do a vegan
  cinnamon snail (kanelsnegl). Toddler verdict: the single greatest food
  on earth, apparently.
- **Lunch:** vegan smørrebrød exists and it's beautiful — rye bread open
  sandwiches a small human can dismantle piece by piece.
- **Dinner:** the plant-based fine-ish dining scene is strong, and Danes
  eat early, so a 5:30pm seating with a toddler is completely normal.
  Bless this country.

## Supermarket cheat sheet

Netto and Irma have huge own-brand plant ranges, all clearly labeled
(look for the green V). Oat milk costs less than in most of Europe —
someone finally got the memo.

## The verdict

**Difficulty: 2/5.** Everything works, everyone's kind, every café has a
high chair. The only pain is the prices — expect London-and-then-some.
The cargo bike is non-negotiable.
